## Research 1｜Demand

The current note already targets a real transition: users are moving from ad-hoc prompting toward agent-like workflows with delegated tasks, tools and access. The public page shows 2 visible likes, which is a human-interest signal but not purchase evidence.

Current external evidence also supports the importance of governance and workflow design rather than simply adding agents:

- OpenAI describes production agent deployment as requiring technical boundaries, approval for higher-risk actions, and telemetry / auditability.
  https://openai.com/index/running-codex-safely/
- OpenAI Presence is described as combining models with policies, permissions, evaluation, monitoring, escalation and human judgment for governed production workflows.
  https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/
- McKinsey 2026 commentary on agentic organizations argues that value realization depends on redesigning workflows, leadership and operating models rather than technology alone.
  https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/people-and-organization/our-insights/ai-is-everywhere-the-agentic-organization-isnt-yet

Demand conclusion:
**PASS**. The paid need is not “how to make an AI employee.” It is how to define job scope, permissions, evidence, escalation and stop conditions so an agent can operate without uncontrolled autonomy.

## Research 3｜Red Team / Legal / Policy

### Avoid anthropomorphic overclaiming

Do not claim:
- AI is legally an employee
- AI has responsibility in the legal / employment sense
- AI can replace human accountability

Use:
- “社員のように役割分担して運用する”
- “agent role / operating responsibility within the workflow”

### Avoid autonomy guarantees

Do not claim:
- 24/7 unattended operation is always safe or possible
- human approval can be removed from high-risk actions
- failures can always be automatically recovered

Use:
- “low-risk actions may be automated where the connected system and policy allow”
- “higher-risk actions should have explicit approval / escalation rules”

### Revenue claim

The article title and body may discuss connecting AI use to revenue outcomes, but should not imply that agentization itself causes revenue. Revenue remains a market outcome verified only by purchase / contract / reward / commission evidence.

### Platform quality

This update must add real operational value: Role Charter, Permission Matrix, Escalation, Evidence Contract, rollback and outcome review. Do not inflate word count with generic agent definitions.

Red Team conclusion:
**PASS WITH CONSTRAINTS**.
