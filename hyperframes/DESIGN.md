# Stratum Praxis HyperFrames Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
Dark, precise, premium operational-tech visual language. Minimal rather than flashy. High contrast black canvas, silver typography, restrained mint accent, subtle grid and glow. Motion should feel controlled, useful and businesslike: short reveals, clean vertical movement, no chaotic effects.

## Colors
- Background: `#090b0e`
- Soft background: `#10141a`
- Panel: `#131820`
- Line: `#2a313b`
- Primary text: `#f3f5f7`
- Silver headline: `#d6dbe1`
- Muted text: `#a7b0bc`
- Accent / CTA: `#66e3c4`
- Accent dark: `#102d28`

## Typography
- Inter
- ui-sans-serif / system-ui fallback

## Motion
- Controlled upward reveals
- Small scale changes only
- Short fades and clean wipes
- Strong pauses around the value proposition and CTA
- Avoid continuous decorative motion

## What NOT to Do
- No rainbow gradients
- No neon cyberpunk overload
- No generic blue SaaS palette
- No bouncing/cartoon motion
- No excessive particle effects
- No unverified savings or revenue claims
