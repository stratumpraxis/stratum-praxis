5 · 30-Day Validation Plan

Week 1 — Problem validation

Define one buyer/problem/outcome; collect 10 real problem statements; map 3 alternatives; write the smallest promise; create a one-page sample; show it only where rules allow; record objections.

Week 2 — Build one useful product

Create the core guide, reusable asset, preview/sample, QA checklist, clean filenames and delivery backup. Do not create a catalog.

Week 3 — Publish + qualified traffic

Publish the product page; use 3–5 proof-based pieces of content; participate in real buyer conversations; use one searchable/free asset if available.

Week 4 — Diagnose

  • Low visits → distribution.
  • Visits but low checkout starts → promise, preview, proof or fit.
  • Checkout starts but low purchases → price, trust or purchase friction.
  • Purchases → repeat the same buyer/problem/channel before expanding.

  • □ Mobile page is readable.

8 · Simple economics

Gross revenue = purchases × price

Net before tax ≈ gross revenue − platform/payment fees − refunds − paid acquisition − directly attributable tool cost.

Paid-tool break-even: monthly tool cost ÷ net contribution per sale = additional sales required to justify the tool.
