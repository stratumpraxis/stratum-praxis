Use it in 3 minutes

  1. Capture the exact income claim, not your interpretation.
  2. Score what evidence is actually shown.
  3. Mark the hidden requirements the creator disclosed.
  4. Use the verdict to decide whether to ignore, research, or run a bounded test.
  5. If you test, define a stop condition before spending more money or time.
The goal is not to prove every creator wrong. The goal is to stop copying a headline before you understand the mechanism and dependencies behind it.

Verdicts

TEST SMALL — enough evidence and reproducibility to justify a bounded experiment.

VERIFY FIRST — interesting signal, but missing evidence or key economics.

WATCH — plausible idea, weak proof or heavy hidden dependencies.

IGNORE — little decision value right now.

AVOID — high-risk / misleading structure or trading-style claim with weak evidence.

This is a decision-support tool, not financial, investment, legal, tax, or business advice. It does not independently verify claims or guarantee outcomes.
