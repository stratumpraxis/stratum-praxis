# AX.cvel — Brand & Publication Spec

## Public identity
Display name: `AX.cvel`

Working pronunciation: `A-X cvel` / `エーエックス・シーヴェル` until the owner chooses a spoken form.

## Brand role
Practical problem-solving media for AI, work systems, tool choice, cost control and useful automation.

## Editorial promise
Every piece must answer a concrete question or remove a concrete friction point.

## Visual direction
- restrained, editorial, technical;
- dark / neutral surfaces are acceptable;
- typography and information hierarchy matter more than decorative symbols;
- avoid generic glowing-AI imagery as a default;
- show the actual problem, decision, comparison or workflow whenever possible;
- mobile-first captions and readable safe zones.

## Video signature
Opening should establish the problem immediately. No long logo animation.

Preferred sequence:
`problem -> mechanism -> decision rule -> useful next step`

End card may use `AX.cvel` as a compact wordmark. Keep it under ~1 second for Shorts unless the story needs more.

## Content separation
- AX.cvel: practical question solving / decision media.
- Forwelle: broader AI/technology/systems storytelling.
- Stratum Praxis: B2B commercial/governance/ROI brand.

Do not publish the same edit across all three.

## Rights and claims
- Use original or commercially safe visuals/audio.
- Do not imply sponsorship or affiliation without evidence.
- Do not claim a certification, government recognition, guaranteed savings, guaranteed income or human review unless true and documented.
- AI assistance may be used, but final videos must contain original editorial value and must not be mass-produced filler.

## Account constraints
YouTube phone verification is not confirmed. Production must remain viable without advanced channel features. Do not store credentials or recovery information in this repository.

## First scale rule
Do not scale beyond 3 high-quality videos/week until at least one of these is observed:
- repeatable retention improvement;
- meaningful outbound clicks;
- subscriber conversion;
- verified downstream purchase/activation signal.
