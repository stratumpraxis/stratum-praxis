## Buffer strategy

Do not create multiple Buffer accounts just for organizational neatness. Add a new free Buffer account/workspace only when all are true:
1. the brand lane is approved above;
2. its platform account exists and is safe to operate;
3. the current Buffer connection limit would otherwise block an independent lane;
4. the lane has a distinct topic/audience/revenue thesis;
5. credentials and recovery are controlled by the owner;
6. the official channel connection is verified before any automated publish attempt.

Each Buffer workspace must map to one explicit brand lane. The publishing engine must never guess which same-platform account to use.

## Parallelization rule

Parallel growth means separate editorial businesses, not duplicate-post farms. The engine may operate multiple lanes on the same day only when each selected topic independently passes demand, rights, safety, originality and monetization gates. No requirement exists to fill every account every day.
