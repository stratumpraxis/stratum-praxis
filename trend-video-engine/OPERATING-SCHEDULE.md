# Trend Video Engine — Operating Schedule

Last updated: 2026-08-26 JST

## Principle

Do not publish the same edit everywhere and do not force a post at every run. The engine wakes at fixed decision windows, then each platform team independently decides whether to research, prepare, publish, or skip.

## Active connected lanes

### Stratum Praxis TikTok — English
- Role: discovery, follower growth, future Creator Rewards path.
- Default formats: GROWTH CLIP 20–45s; REWARDS-READY 60–90s when the topic can sustain it.
- Starting posting windows while account-native analytics are still thin:
  - Monday: around 13:00 JST test window.
  - Tuesday: early-morning test only when a strong scheduled candidate exists; otherwise reserve for late-evening testing.
  - Wednesday: around 22:00 JST preferred.
  - Thursday: around 13:00 or 22:00 JST.
  - Friday: around 18:00–22:00 JST.
  - Saturday: around 15:00–17:00 JST.
  - Sunday: around 09:00–13:00 JST.
- These are benchmark starting points from 2026 Buffer aggregate data, not permanent truth. Replace with TikTok Studio follower-active-time data when available.
- Normal cap: 0–2 posts/day. A third post requires a genuinely separate high-velocity trend and monetization/originality QA approval.

### Forwelle YouTube — English
- Role: subscriber acquisition plus YPP path.
- Shorts: discovery and subscriber conversion.
- Long-form: 3–8 minute original explainers derived from proven Short topics when justified, to build qualified public watch hours.
- Starting Shorts windows:
  - Main window: 18:00–21:00 JST.
  - Stronger benchmark days: Thu–Sat; Friday is the anchor test day.
  - Wednesday: around 19:00–21:00 JST.
- Long-form starting windows:
  - Main: morning 08:00–11:00 JST.
  - Stronger benchmark days: Sunday, Tuesday, Monday.
  - Publish 1–2 hours before known audience peak once YouTube Studio provides enough data.
- Normal cap: 0–2 Shorts/day; long-form is not added just to increase volume.
- Every five uploads run the human-reviewer originality test.

### Stratum Praxis Instagram — English support lane
- Role: credibility, secondary discovery, profile/site routing; not the main monetization engine.
- Preferred Reels window: 18:00–23:00 JST, with Wednesday/Thursday preferred.
- Normal cadence: 3–5 feed/Reels posts per week rather than daily forced volume.
- Only post when the visual/caption is genuinely suitable for Instagram; do not automatically mirror every TikTok.

### daily_item_log — commerce / Rakuten lane
- Separate monetization model: useful product discovery → compliant affiliate/ROOM route.

- Start only when official posting connection and compliant revenue route are verified.

## Four daily decision windows

The engine runs at approximately 08:00, 13:00, 19:00 and 22:00 JST.

### 08:00 — Research + long-form window
- Refresh global trend radar and previous-post signals.
- Prefer YouTube long-form preparation/publication on strong long-form days.
- Do not force TikTok unless the current weekday/time is a strong test slot or a time-sensitive trend is decaying quickly.

### 13:00 — Midday TikTok/test window
- Re-check trend velocity and competing narratives.
- TikTok may publish on strong weekday slots such as Monday/Thursday or when trend urgency justifies it.
- Otherwise prepare assets and hold.

### 19:00 — Shorts / Reels primary window
- Primary YouTube Shorts evaluation/publication window.
- Instagram Reels may publish when the creative is native to Instagram.
- TikTok may publish on Friday/evening-strength days or a strong independent trend.

### 22:00 — TikTok late window + learning loop
- Primary late-evening TikTok test on relevant weekdays.
- Review all same-day publication states and public evidence.
- Do not create a duplicate post if an earlier Buffer state is sending/unknown; verify read-only.

## Research and analytics roles

Each lane has:
- Market Radar: current search/social demand and topic velocity.
- Audience Behavior: posting-time tests, views, watch/retention when available, shares/saves/comments, subscriber/follower movement, audience geography/time patterns.
- Platform Editor: hook, duration, pacing, CTA, title/caption optimized specifically for the platform.
- Motion Design: near-future kinetic execution without strobe/flicker/eye strain.
- Rights/Safety Auditor: rights provenance, policy risk, factual verification.
- Monetization Auditor: channel-level monetization eligibility and reusable-content risk.
- Originality Auditor: prevents interchangeable template output.
- Contrarian Reviewer: attacks weak assumptions before publication.
- Publication Verifier: requires sent/public URL evidence, not just a green workflow.
- Learning Lead: transfers one clear win/failure into the next edit.

## Data hierarchy

1. Account-native analytics (TikTok Studio / YouTube Studio / Instagram Insights) when available.
2. Actual post-level public results and Buffer publication evidence.
3. Current large-sample platform benchmarks as a temporary starting point.

Do not overfit to one post. Change one major variable at a time when testing timing, hook, length, or visual rhythm.
