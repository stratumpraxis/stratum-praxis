## Canonical social distribution channel

Official X account: `@ordlume`

Canonical URL: `https://x.com/ordlume`

Use X primarily for:

- new utility launch announcements
- meaningful product improvements
- useful standalone tips derived from the utility itself

Do **not** turn the account into a donation directory, support-request feed, or cross-promotion loop between unrelated support asks.

The X account is a distribution channel, not the parent brand of every utility. Standalone utilities do not need a permanent visible X link unless it improves the page for the user.

## Boundary

Do **not** use this address or X identity as the default contact / social identity for the Stratum B2B site, B2B audits, enterprise buyer routes, or other project brands unless explicitly reassigned.

## Public UX rule

The operating email is **not a default public UI element**.

Do not add a contact strip, operator directory, social strip, or email CTA merely for consistency. A standalone utility should stay focused on:

`useful result → next action → optional support`

Only expose a contact address or social link when a real user need, legal requirement, partnership route, explicit support workflow, or genuinely useful update channel makes it necessary.

## Revenue integrity

Preferred monetization for these utilities is transparent optional support / tip / one-time contribution after useful value has already been delivered.

Rules:

- free result is available without payment
- support does not change a score or diagnostic result
- support does not unlock public-safety information
- do not imply donations go to disaster victims, charities, public agencies, or third parties unless that is literally true
- describe what the money supports on that specific page
- do not route one support CTA into a carousel of unrelated donation asks
- keep payment destinations distinguishable by utility when practical

## SEO / distribution

Search discovery may be shared through the same domain infrastructure, but each utility should be measured and optimized separately by its own search intent, landing page, and user value.

X may drive traffic to each utility independently. Do not use a generic multi-donation landing page as the default destination from X.
