# Forwelle — YouTube 10K Growth & Monetization Research

Updated: 2026-09-01 JST

## Core model

YouTube's own recommendation guidance groups performance into three buckets:

**Appeal → Engagement → Satisfaction**

For operating decisions, Forwelle will use:

**Growth = Topic Demand × Packaging × Hook × Retention × Satisfaction × Repeatability**

**Revenue = Qualified Audience × Watch Depth × Monetization Mix × Policy Eligibility**

## 30 researched requirements

1. **Start with audience demand, not production.** Frame AI topics around a problem, consequence, surprise, or useful change that a broader viewer can understand.
2. **Packaging is part of the content.** Long-form titles/thumbnails must make one clear promise; Shorts must make the first frame perform the packaging job.
3. **Win the first seconds.** The opening must immediately deliver the promised subject, conflict, result, or surprising visual.
4. **Avoid click/promise mismatch.** Appeal without delivery damages engagement and satisfaction.
5. **Optimize for sustained attention, not raw clicks.** YouTube explicitly evaluates whether viewers stick around after choosing a video.
6. **Engineer narrative progression.** Every section should create a reason to watch the next section: setup → consequence → evidence → payoff.
7. **Use pattern interrupts deliberately.** Visual or semantic changes every few seconds can refresh attention, but should clarify the story rather than add noise.
8. **Satisfaction is the final gate.** The viewer should leave with a useful insight, emotional payoff, or resolved curiosity.
9. **Use video-level analytics, not intuition alone.** Diagnose each upload from actual viewer behavior.
10. **Compare retention against your own typical videos.** YouTube provides typical-retention comparisons for recent videos of similar length; internal baselines are more useful than generic viral benchmarks.
11. **For Shorts, track Stayed to watch.** This is the share that watches instead of swiping away after the initial seconds.
12. **Track Engaged views.** Shorts engaged views measure viewers who stay past the initial seconds, excluding loops.
13. **Track Average View Duration.** This measures watch depth among engaged Shorts viewers.
14. **Track Average Percentage Viewed.** This normalizes retention for length and exposes pacing problems.
15. **Treat rewatch/looping as a design benefit, not a trick.** Endings that naturally reconnect to the opening can increase repeat viewing without degrading viewer value.
16. **Match Short length to the idea.** Remove padding; add seconds only when they increase understanding or payoff.
17. **Use Shorts primarily for discovery/subscriber acquisition.** They can build reach rapidly, but Shorts-feed watch time does not count toward the 4,000-hour long-form YPP route.
18. **Use long-form to build watch hours and deeper intent.** Convert winning Short topics into 3–8 minute explanations when the subject can sustain curiosity.
19. **For long-form, track impressions and CTR.** CTR is the first packaging diagnostic, not the final success metric.
20. **Audit the first 30 seconds of long-form.** Intro retention shows whether the title/thumbnail promise was actually delivered.
21. **Move top moments earlier.** If a later section creates unusually high retention, test bringing that value forward.
22. **Study spikes.** Rewatch/share spikes identify concepts, examples, or visuals worth expanding into future videos.
23. **Cut dips.** Retention drops reveal confusion, delay, repetitive explanation, weak visuals, or promise mismatch.
24. **Use YouTube thumbnail Test & Compare for public long-form.** Test up to three thumbnails; YouTube evaluates winners using watch-time share. It is not available for private videos or Shorts.
25. **Target the current full YPP threshold before the 2027 change.** As of 2026-09-01: 1,000 subscribers + either 4,000 qualified public long-form watch hours in 12 months or 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days.
26. **Use early monetization at 500 subscribers where eligible.** 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads in 90 days + either 3,000 qualified watch hours or 3M qualified Shorts views unlocks eligible fan-funding/Shopping features.
27. **Plan for the Feb 1, 2027 threshold increase.** New YPP applicants will need 1,000 subscribers + either 8,000 qualified watch hours or 20M qualified Shorts views; reaching full YPP before then has strategic value.
28. **Understand revenue-share economics.** Current official shares: Watch Page Ads 55% of net ad revenue; Shorts 45% of allocated Creator Pool revenue; memberships/Supers/Thanks 70% of net revenue.
29. **Do not build a mass-produced AI channel.** YouTube's inauthentic-content policy rejects repetitive, generic, templated, mass-produced content. Reusable production infrastructure is fine only when the substance is materially varied and original.
30. **Disclose realistic synthetic media and keep originality visible.** YouTube says AI disclosure itself does not reduce audience reach or monetization eligibility. Forwelle should use original narration/editorial structure, rights-safe visuals, primary sources, and explicit synthetic-media disclosure when required.

## Forwelle operating law

### Content pillars
1. AI agents / automation frontier
2. AI security / safety
3. Practical implications / workflows

### Shorts decision loop
Topic opportunity → one-sentence thesis → first-second hook → 25–40s narrative → original visual system → QA → private upload → public gate → 24h/72h/7d metrics → KEEP / ITERATE / DROP

### Required metrics
- Views
- Engaged views
- Stayed to watch
- Average view duration
- Average percentage viewed
- Subscribers gained
- Subscribers per 1,000 views
- For long-form: impressions, CTR, first-30s retention, total watch hours

### Monetization ladder
- **0–499 subs:** audience/product fit; no dependence on ad revenue
- **500+ subs:** early YPP/fan funding/Shopping where eligibility is met
- **1,000+ subs:** full current YPP after 4,000 hours or 10M Shorts views
- **10,000 subs:** optimize revenue per viewer through long-form, affiliates/Shopping, partnerships, and owned offers where channel fit and policy eligibility are confirmed
