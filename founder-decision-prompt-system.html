FAQ

Before you buy

Is this a pack of generic prompts?

No. It is three structured decision workflows with inputs, audit criteria, explicit uncertainty handling, scored outputs and next-test rules.

Which AI models can I use?

Designed for capable general-purpose models such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Current-market claims still need live research or independent verification.

Who is it for?

Founders, solo operators, consultants, agencies, product builders and creators making real market, offer or funnel decisions.

What is it not?

It is not market data, a guarantee of demand, legal or financial advice, a done-for-you growth service, or permission to invent missing metrics.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes for the purchaser's own business or client-delivery work. The prompt text itself may not be republished, redistributed or sold as a competing prompt product.
