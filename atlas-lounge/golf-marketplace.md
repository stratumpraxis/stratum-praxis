# Atlas Privilege — Golf marketplace proof

## Thesis
Do not operate a golf club, concierge or travel agency. Own the high-quality discovery/filtering surface; let the golf course, resort or authorized booking provider own inventory, booking, payment, cancellation and fulfilment.

## Initial supply classes
Only include offers that are public or explicitly authorized:
- tee-time booking providers;
- golf resorts and stay-and-play packages;
- tournament spectator packages from official/authorized sellers;
- premium golf travel partners;
- equipment/benefit partners where affiliate or member-benefit terms are explicit.

## Member value
A quiet, self-service layer for affluent users:
- saved regions/dates;
- golf + hotel + transport context;
- cancellation/booking-condition visibility;
- curated premium opportunities;
- later: early alerts or verified partner benefits.

## Revenue order
1. Approved affiliate/referral commission.
2. Clearly labelled featured placement that cannot buy editorial ranking.
3. Membership only after recurring supply/value is proven.
4. Partner benefit economics after a real agreement exists.

## Responsibility boundary
Atlas does not claim private-course access without rights, does not accept booking money for third parties, does not guarantee availability, and does not mediate disputes as the base model. Users complete transactions with the named provider.

## Proof test
Before building a standalone site, obtain at least one real, terms-compatible supply path and measure outbound booking intent from the existing Atlas staging surface. If no compliant supply path exists, keep the lane incubated rather than manufacturing exclusivity.
