# AI & SaaS Spend Decision Kit — Buyer Guide

## Goal
Turn recurring AI and SaaS spend into explicit, reviewable decisions instead of automatic renewals.

## Workflow
1. Inventory the tools you want to review.
2. Enter approximate monthly cost, usage, workflow importance, overlap risk and renewal urgency in the purchased workspace.
3. Export the decision register.
4. Verify the highest-cost non-KEEP candidates against actual usage, owner, dependencies, contract and renewal timing.
5. Choose the smallest reversible action first.
6. Record the pre-change baseline and re-measure after 30 days.

## Decision labels
- KEEP — current evidence supports maintaining the subscription.
- REDUCE — keep the capability while testing fewer seats, credits or a lower tier.
- CONSOLIDATE — investigate replacing overlapping capabilities with fewer tools.
- REVIEW — gather evidence before renewal or another purchase.
- CANCEL — candidate only when usage and workflow importance are both weak; verify dependencies before acting.

## 30-day operating cadence
### Days 1–3
Build the register and identify the top five annual-cost candidates.

### Days 4–10
Verify usage, owner, dependencies, renewal timing and alternatives.

### Days 11–20
Take one or more reversible actions where evidence is sufficient.

### Days 21–30
Measure actual cost change and operational impact. Reverse changes that damage workflow value.

Use the $499 AI & SaaS Spend Waste Audit when the decision value is large enough to justify independent review, the stack is too complex for a self-service pass, or the evidence is contradictory.
