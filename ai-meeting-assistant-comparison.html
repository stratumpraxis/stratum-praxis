Before you add another AI meeting assistant, decide what job it is replacing.

Meeting tools are easy to stack: video platform, transcription, summaries, sales intelligence and noise cleanup can overlap. The useful question is not “which tool has the most AI?” but “which paid capability should remain after this purchase?”

The overlap check that matters before checkout

1. Existing suite coverageDoes Zoom, Teams, Webex or another paid suite already provide acceptable summaries or transcription?
2. Real adoptionHow many seats actively review summaries, search transcripts or push action items into work systems?
3. Duplicate captureAre two tools recording or summarizing the same meetings?
4. Workflow completionDo notes actually create CRM updates, tasks or decisions, or do they simply create more text?
Buying rule: if the new tool does not replace an existing paid layer, remove measurable admin work, or improve a revenue/decision workflow, treat it as incremental spend until proven otherwise.
