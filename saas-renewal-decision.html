What to inspect before signing or renewing

  • Actual active use versus paid seats, credits, tiers, and commitments.
  • Duplicate capability across AI assistants, meeting tools, automation, analytics, storage, security, or collaboration products.
  • Whether the workflow supported by the tool has measurable operational or revenue value.
  • Whether a shorter contract, smaller plan, or phased purchase would preserve flexibility.
  • Whether variable AI/token spend has an owner, budget, anomaly threshold, and stop condition.
  • Whether the renewal date has a named decision owner and a date before auto-renewal becomes irreversible.
