Decision support, not a guarantee

Calculators, diagnostics, checklists, research utilities and other public tools are provided to organize inputs and make trade-offs more visible. Outputs are not promises of savings, revenue, performance, legal compliance or business results.

Financial and readiness utilities

Money Resilience and related utilities are educational decision-support tools. They do not provide individualized investment, tax or financial advice, do not recommend buying or selling a financial asset and do not predict future prices. Household-readiness tools likewise organize preparedness information rather than guarantee safety or availability.

Digital products and professional services

For a paid digital product, the relevant product page and checkout route describe the current price and intended scope. For a professional audit or recurring service, the relevant service page describes the current deliverable, scope and operating model. Product-specific terms shown at purchase or delivery control where they are more specific than this general page.

Before paying, review the current product or service page. Do not rely on an old screenshot, repost or third-party description as the current offer.

Third-party services

Payments, downloads, external data and outbound links can involve third-party services. Their availability, terms and processing are controlled by those providers. Stratum does not promise uninterrupted availability of a third-party platform.

Acceptable use

Do not use Stratum tools or materials to misrepresent evidence, fabricate customer proof, violate another party's rights, bypass access controls or carry out unlawful activity. Licences supplied with downloadable systems or code govern reuse of those specific assets.

Evidence boundary: a calculated scenario, sample output or public bounty/reward is not the same thing as a verified business outcome or settled payment. The site deliberately keeps those states separate.

Changes and current offer

Features, prices and public routes may change as the products evolve. The current live page at the time of use or purchase is the relevant public description. Material privacy behavior is described separately in the Privacy page.
