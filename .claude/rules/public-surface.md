# Stratum Praxis — Public Revenue Surface Memo

Updated: 2026-09-11

## Rule

The canonical public Stratum surface is the revenue-focused `sitemap.xml` only.

A page that is not in the canonical public sitemap must NOT be re-added to the homepage, main navigation, Live Lab, Product Router, revenue-cycle slider, or other public discovery surfaces unless it is explicitly promoted back into the revenue funnel.

Do not delete excluded assets merely because they are excluded from the public surface. Preserve them as research, legacy, experimental, delivery, or internal assets as appropriate.

## Revenue-surface test

Before adding any page to the public surface, require at least one of the following:

- creates qualified evidence that routes directly to a current paid offer;
- contains an active direct checkout for a current offer;
- is a specialist paid service page;
- is a recurring revenue / retention page;
- is legally required support content.

If none apply, keep the page out of the public revenue surface.
