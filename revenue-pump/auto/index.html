01 · AUTOMATIC, NOT MAGIC

It scores what it can observe.

Headings, useful depth, decision language, controls, calls to action and value-route signals. It cannot know real buyer intent from HTML alone.

02 · REWORK ≠ LOW SCORE

A bad page is not automatically valuable.

Worth Reworking rises when Problem/Answer foundations exist but downstream Decision/Action/Revenue layers are weak.

03 · HUMAN REVIEW

Use evidence before escalation.

Traffic quality, actual queries, offers, prices and purchase behavior belong in a paid Audit only when deeper judgment is useful.

What this does not promise.

The Auto Scan does not predict or guarantee revenue, profit, search rankings, traffic or conversion lift. It is a fast structural triage tool for deciding whether an existing web asset deserves a closer look and what to test next.
