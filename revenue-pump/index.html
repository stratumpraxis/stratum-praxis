THE METHOD

Useful first. Revenue second.

Revenue Pump is not permission to add more CTAs. It is a way to find the smallest useful next connection.

01 / PROBLEM

Capture the real blockage

Start from the visitor, not the catalog.

02 / ANSWER

Give value first

Answer, calculate, diagnose or clarify.

03 / DECISION

Reduce uncertainty

Help choose, compare, qualify or stop.

04 / ACTION

Make one move obvious

One useful next step beats five equal CTAs.

05 / REVENUE

Let value accumulate

Paid, Lead, Affiliate, Support, Share or Return — only where it fits.

What this does not promise.

Revenue Pump Score and Worth Reworking are structural heuristics. They do not predict or guarantee revenue, profit, traffic, search rankings or conversion lift. Real commercial decisions become stronger when traffic quality, buyer intent, offer, price, distribution and purchase evidence are available.
