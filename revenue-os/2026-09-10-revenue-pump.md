Date: 2026-09-10

Public route: https://stratumpraxis.com/revenue-pump/

## Purpose

Revenue Pump is the Stratum Praxis mechanism for turning an existing website, URL, article, free resource, or dormant web asset into a clearer value path:

Problem → Answer → Decision → Action → Revenue

Revenue is an outcome layer, not a guarantee. The mechanism must not promise revenue, rankings, traffic, or conversion uplift.

## One-site product shape

1. Free Revenue Pump Scan
2. Revenue Pump Score
3. Worth Reworking score
4. Primary Block
5. Recommended transformation
6. Recommended next test
7. One primary value route + one alternative
8. Revenue Pump Audit escalation
9. Revenue Pump Blueprint escalation

Primary route types may include Paid, Lead, Affiliate, Support, Share, or Return. Do not force checkout when the page is not ready.

## URL guard

`/revenue-pump/` is assigned to Revenue Pump and should not be reused or merged into unrelated utilities.

The household readiness route remains separate:
https://stratumpraxis.com/72-hour-household-readiness/

## Operating rule

Use Revenue Pump to prioritize existing assets before creating more pages. The core question is not “what can we add?” but “where does the current useful asset stop, and what is the smallest next test that can connect it to a legitimate value route?”

## Analytics and privacy

Reuse `/scos-analytics.js` for scan start, scan completion, score, primary block, Worth Reworking score, sharing, and CTA interest. Do not send the entered target URL as an analytics property. The URL may be included only when the user explicitly opens the audit request email.
