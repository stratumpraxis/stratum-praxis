# Multi-Platform Video Distribution Engine — 2026-08-28

## Objective
Turn each high-quality original video into multiple platform-native distribution assets without becoming a duplicate-post farm or weakening monetization eligibility.

## Core brand lanes
- Forwelle — YouTube accumulation / monetization priority.
- AX.cvel — practical AI/workflow short-form acquisition media.
- Vector Praxis — Japanese-language practical AI/business media.

## Primary distribution surfaces

### Buffer-managed / API-friendly
Use Buffer as the preferred central publishing layer where connected and permitted:
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels / Instagram video
- Facebook Pages / Reels-compatible video
- YouTube Shorts
- Threads video
- Bluesky video
- Pinterest video
- X video where operationally appropriate

Do not assume a channel is connected. Every publishing target must have an explicit verified account mapping.

### Separate lanes
- Snapchat Spotlight — separate official/approved publishing route; do not fake automation through browser abuse.
- Rumble — separate upload/publishing lane; prioritize winning topics and longer-form repackaging when it better fits monetization conditions.

## Content transformation rule
One master idea may produce multiple versions, but each platform version should be intentional.

Required adaptations where relevant:
- aspect ratio / safe zone
- hook wording
- duration
- caption density
- CTA wording
- platform-native title/description
- music/audio rights
- cover/thumbnail

Do not add third-party watermarks to platform uploads.
Do not mechanically duplicate the same file across many brand accounts.

## Initial master formats

### Short acquisition master
- 25–45 seconds
- 9:16
- strong first 1.5 seconds
- 5–9 meaningful visual beats
- clear editorial value
- captions
- one CTA after value

Use for:
- AX.cvel TikTok
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook Reels
- Snapchat Spotlight where eligible
- YouTube Shorts / Forwelle only if the topic fits the Forwelle audience

### Monetization / depth master
- 60–90 seconds for short-form platforms where longer original content is rewarded or useful
- 3–8 minutes for YouTube / Rumble only after a short-form topic demonstrates demand

## Publishing state machine
Every post must move through:
1. DRAFT
2. QA_READY
3. ACCOUNT_VERIFIED
4. READY_TO_PUBLISH
5. PUBLISHED
6. VERIFIED_PUBLIC
7. METRICS_COLLECTING
8. WINNER / ITERATE / RETIRE

Never mark PUBLISHED based only on a queued or upload response. Verify the live public post URL or platform-confirmed published state where available.

## Account mapping gate
Before any autonomous publish:
- exact platform
- exact account handle/channel ID
- exact brand lane
- language
- account ownership/authorization
- API/Buffer channel connection status
- feature restrictions

If two same-platform accounts could match, stop rather than guess.

## Safety / integrity
- no fake engagement
- no follow-for-follow automation
- no view manipulation
- no mass comments or unsolicited DMs
- no CAPTCHA bypass
- no identity challenge bypass
- no high-frequency retry loops
- no unlicensed media
- no misleading earnings or performance claims
- no duplicate content farms

On platform warning, CAPTCHA, identity check, permission loss or uncertain target account: stop automated publishing and require re-verification.

## Performance loop
Measure per platform where available:
- impressions/views
- first-seconds retention
- average percentage viewed
- completion rate
- saves/shares/comments
- profile visit
- outbound CTA click
- landing page view
- checkout start
- verified purchase
- activation

Winner topics should receive:
1. stronger second short version;
2. platform-native rewrite rather than duplicate repost;
3. Forwelle long-form expansion when relevant;
4. increased distribution only after real downstream evidence.
