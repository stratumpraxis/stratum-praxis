# Signal Praxis — Authority / Sponsor / Membership Cell — 2026-08-26

## Objective
Move from only selling products to owning a decision surface where relevant AI/SaaS brands want placement and readers may pay for recurring decision intelligence.

## Parallel roles
- Market / Capital Mapper: find where AI/SaaS marketing budgets, platform leverage, credits, listings and sponsorship demand already exist.
- Sponsor Demand Architect: package inbound commercial inventory without outbound-heavy sales.
- Membership Architect: package recurring value, not an endless content library.
- Authority Builder: accumulate only verifiable third-party proof; never fabricate awards, reviews or reach.
- Measurement Repair: make qualified traffic and conversion observable before publishing audience claims.
- Distribution Engineer: grow qualified traffic through search, directories, feeds, marketplaces and other existing-demand surfaces.
- Editorial Trust Auditor: keep sponsored money separate from rankings, verdicts and factual conclusions.
- Stability Keeper: do not keep redesigning before enough observations exist.

## Counterpart rule
If one role stalls, create one temporary counterpart specialized in that exact weakness. Examples: weak measurement -> Analytics Engineer; weak organic reach -> Search Distribution Specialist; weak sponsor offer -> Media Buyer Counterpart; weak recurring value -> Retention Architect; weak proof -> Third-Party Validation Scout. Dissolve the extra role after the bottleneck clears; do not grow permanent bureaucracy.

## Implemented
- `signal/partners.html`: inbound Featured Placement / Sponsored Brief / Research Underwriting surface with editorial firewall and partner-request tracking.
- `signal/membership.html`: recurring-value architecture for Decision Briefs / Renewal & Price Watch / Opportunity Radar / Member Decision Tools. No invented public price; interest click tracked.
- `sitemap.xml`: added both surfaces for discoverability.

## Evidence / current bottleneck
PostHog current project shows no standard `$pageview` events in the last 30 days, while `funnel_view` recorded 48 events from 3 visitors on 2026-08-24 and 5 events from 3 visitors on 2026-08-25 for `stratumpraxis.com`. Therefore public sponsor traffic claims are not allowed yet. First bottleneck is measurement consistency plus qualified traffic, not cosmetic site work.

## Authority rule
Use existing public proof assets and legitimate third-party selection channels. Awards, store acceptance, media features, hackathon results, reviews and adoption count only when a third party actually confirms them. Reputation OS remains the evidence ledger. Do not buy fake awards or imply pending submissions are wins.

## Commercial model
1. Public Signal feed attracts decision-intent traffic.
2. Decision tools/comparisons create utility and search surfaces.
3. Relevant brands can request clearly labeled commercial placement.
4. Sponsorship never purchases recommendation or ranking.
5. Paid membership opens only when recurring fulfillment cadence and billing terms are fixed.
6. Verified traffic / partner interest / member interest determine the next bottleneck.

## Hold / kill rules
- Do not invent traffic, audience size, customers, awards or sponsor demand.
- Do not set or change live membership pricing without explicit pricing instruction.
- Do not create more media brands while Signal Praxis can absorb the opportunity.
- Do not run rapid duplicate outreach or high-frequency platform checks.
- No redesign loop unless a measurable bottleneck justifies it.
