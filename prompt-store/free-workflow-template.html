A reusable structure for turning an ambiguous task into steps, ownership, completion criteria, failure handling and QA. Free to adapt for your own work.

Copy this template
TASK: [task]
GOAL: [goal]
CONTEXT: [context]
AVAILABLE TOOLS: [tools]
CONSTRAINTS: [constraints]

Return:
1. OBJECTIVE — rewrite the task as one precise operational objective.
2. REQUIRED INPUTS — required / optional / missing.
3. WORKFLOW STEPS — Action, Owner, Input, Output, Tool, Completion condition.
4. DECISION POINTS — condition, options, recommended default.
5. FAILURE POINTS — likely failure, detection, fallback, escalation.
6. QUALITY CHECK — objective pass/fail criteria.
7. REPEATABLE SOP — compress the workflow into a reusable SOP.
8. AUTOMATION OPPORTUNITIES — safe now / human-reviewed / not worth automating.
9. NEXT ACTION — one immediate action only.

Rules:
- Do not invent access, permissions, data or completed actions.
- Label assumptions.
- Prefer fewer tools and clear ownership.
- Preserve human approval for irreversible, financial, legal, security-sensitive or account-level actions.
- Avoid infinite retries and policy-risk automation.

Why this works better than “make me a workflow.”

Ownership

Each operation has one primary owner instead of “AI handles everything.”

Completion

Every step defines what “done” means, reducing vague handoffs.

Failure handling

Retries, fallbacks and escalation are designed before something breaks.

QA

The workflow ends with observable pass/fail criteria, not confidence.
