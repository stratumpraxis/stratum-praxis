## Reddit / community — value-first draft
Title: I stopped treating prompts as one-off instructions and started designing them like operating procedures

Body:
One change made AI workflows much easier to reuse: I stopped asking only “what should the model say?” and started defining the operating shape around the prompt.

For each workflow I now specify:
- required inputs
- one primary owner per operation
- completion conditions
- decision points
- failure detection and fallback
- QA
- human approval boundaries

A simple example is newsletter publishing. Instead of “write my weekly newsletter,” the system first resolves what is required, who owns research/drafting/review, what must be checked before send, and what can safely be automated.

I turned the same structure into three reusable systems for workflow architecture, tool/role routing, and company knowledge. There is a public sample of the output shape here if useful:
