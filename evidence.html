THE STANDARD

Four rules before money enters the route.

These rules are deliberately simple. They exist so a user can understand what a page is doing before deciding whether to keep going.

01 · INPUTS

No invented ROI

Calculators use user-supplied or explicitly sourced inputs. A missing input stays missing rather than becoming a marketing number.

02 · FIT

Free can be the right end state

If a free diagnostic answers the decision, the route can stop there. A paid layer is not treated as mandatory.

03 · SCOPE

Paid depth must be visible

Price, scope and destination should be clear before checkout. Existing routes are used; fabricated checkout links are not.

04 · BOUNDARY

Claims stop where evidence stops

Stratum does not invent customer counts, savings, rankings, guarantees or testimonials to make a page feel stronger.

CLAIM BOUNDARIES

What we show—and what we refuse to fake.

Trust is not another badge. It is the visible difference between a supported fact, a user input, a sample, and an unsupported claim.

WE CAN SHOW

Verifiable product evidence

  • Public prices and existing checkout routes
  • Free tools that can be used without a sales call
  • Sample deliverables and published system documentation
  • Method, limitations, privacy and terms
  • User-entered calculations and clearly labeled scenarios
WE DO NOT INVENT

Decorative credibility

  • Fake customer logos or testimonials
  • Unverified savings or ROI percentages
  • Artificial urgency or fake scarcity
  • Fabricated live-user counters
  • Performance guarantees without evidence
VERIFY FOR YOURSELF

Open the evidence behind the route.

The strongest trust signal is inspectable output. These pages let a visitor check the tool, sample or operating system directly.
