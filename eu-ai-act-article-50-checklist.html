EU AI Act Article 50: a practical transparency checklist before release.

Article 50 transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026. Use this checklist to identify the operational questions your AI product team should resolve before go-live.

1. Direct AI interaction

  • Does a natural person interact directly with the AI system?
  • Is it obvious to a reasonable person that the interaction is with AI?
  • If disclosure is required, is it visible at the right point in the interaction rather than buried in legal text?

2. AI-generated or manipulated content

  • Does the system generate or manipulate audio, images, video or text?
  • Have you evaluated machine-readable marking and detection requirements?
  • Can you produce evidence showing how the marking method was tested?

3. Deepfakes and public-interest content

  • Could generated or manipulated media appear authentic?
  • Does the workflow publish AI-generated text about matters of public interest?
  • Is there documented human review or editorial control where relevant?
  • Are exemption decisions evidence-backed rather than assumed?

4. Evidence and ownership

  • Have you identified whether you are acting as provider, deployer, or both?
  • Is there an owner for each transparency control?
  • Are disclosure screenshots, sample marked outputs, tests and approvals version-controlled?
  • Is there a release decision: READY / READY WITH CONDITIONS / BLOCKED?
