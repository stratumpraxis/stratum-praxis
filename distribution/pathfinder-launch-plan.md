# Social Revenue Pathfinder — Launch & Operations

Status: implementation live in repository; external social account/OAuth setup remains owner-required.
Date: 2026-08-26

## Page-split operating teams
- Funnel Product: maintain EN/JA Pathfinder and route quality.
- Localization: English is primary global page; Japanese is first localization. Add another language only after traffic/revenue evidence or a clear distribution opportunity.
- SNS Link: connect Metricool analytics to existing Instagram/TikTok/YouTube; onboard Bluesky, Threads, Pinterest as approved expansion surfaces. Facebook Page deferred.
- Distribution: Buffer remains sole autonomous publisher for Instagram/TikTok/YouTube. No duplicate publisher.
- Pinterest Evergreen: convert suitable owned pages into original pins with destination-message match, useful descriptions, safe owned links, and no copyrighted/logo-dependent assets.
- Measurement: use Metricool for social metrics/MCP, PostHog for site events, Stripe for purchase truth. Optimize toward revenue, not likes.
- Safety Audit: verify destination, rights, factual claims, platform fit, disclosures, duplicate risk, and unknown-state retry protection before autonomous distribution.
- Record Keeper: update this plan/provider policy only for material changes, blockers, validated wins, or provider-role changes.

## Revenue loop
Demand signal -> safe original content -> social distribution -> Pathfinder -> closest existing offer -> PostHog funnel evidence -> Stripe purchase -> winner replication.

## Pinterest operating cadence
Start only after Pinterest + Metricool OAuth is complete.
- Initial test: 3-5 distinct evergreen pins/week, not repetitive variants.
- Each pin maps to one relevant owned destination; no generic homepage dumping.
- Rotate destinations across Pathfinder, Revenue Router, ROI Kit, and genuinely relevant free tools/articles.
- Weekly review: saves/outbound clicks -> Pathfinder route clicks -> checkout starts -> purchases.
- Increase volume only when quality and downstream conversion remain acceptable.
- Stop or revise repetitive/low-value concepts; never use copyrighted media, celebrity/real-person likeness, misleading claims, or spammy duplication.

## Localization gate
English + Japanese now. Do not mass-translate. Add a language when at least one applies: meaningful organic traffic from that locale; platform audience evidence; validated purchase/checkout interest; or a low-risk distribution surface with clear fit.

## Manual owner blockers
1. Create/sign in to Metricool Free and complete email/social verification.
2. Create/sign in to Bluesky and accept required terms/verification.
3. Use an Instagram account to create/access Threads and complete Meta authorization.
4. Create/sign in to Pinterest; use a business account if analytics/business features are required; complete required verification/OAuth.
5. In Metricool, connect Instagram/TikTok/YouTube for analytics, then connect approved Bluesky/Threads/Pinterest profiles. Do not enable a duplicate autonomous publishing lane for Instagram/TikTok/YouTube.

## Completion evidence required after manual setup
- Connected-profile list/screenshots or provider confirmation.
- Metricool analytics visible for existing channels.
- First safe Pinterest/Threads/Bluesky distribution item has a valid owned destination.
- PostHog event evidence for Pathfinder visit/route click.
- Stripe remains source of truth for purchase.
