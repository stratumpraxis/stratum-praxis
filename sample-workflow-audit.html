06 · 30-DAY PILOT PLAN

Test, measure, then decide.

The recommendation ends with a reversible sequence so the team can scale, redesign or stop with evidence.

DAYS 1–5Baseline

Measure volume, handling time, exception types and response latency.

DAYS 6–12Shadow mode

Generate suggested actions without sending them. Compare with human decisions.

DAYS 13–21Limited live

Automate only low-risk reminders for a bounded subset with approval checkpoints.

DAYS 22–30Decision

Compare time, error rate, client response and operating cost. Scale, redesign or stop.

Illustrative thresholds: ≥40% reduction in repetitive handling time, no material privacy incident, no increase in incorrect reminders, clear human exception handling, and economics still above operating cost.
