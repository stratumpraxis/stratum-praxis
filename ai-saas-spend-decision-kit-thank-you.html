2. Decision logic

  • KEEP: strong usage + important workflow + low overlap.
  • REDUCE: useful tool, but current tier/seats appear larger than needed.
  • CONSOLIDATE: useful capability with meaningful overlap elsewhere.
  • REVIEW: evidence is weak, renewal is close, or the workflow case is unclear.
  • CANCEL: weak usage and weak workflow importance; verify dependencies before acting.

The score is a prioritization aid, not an instruction to cancel software. Validate contracts, dependencies, security, data retention and operational consequences before changing subscriptions.

3. 30-day action plan

  1. Export the decision register and sort REVIEW / CONSOLIDATE / REDUCE candidates by annual cost.
  2. Verify actual usage, owners, dependencies and renewal dates for the top five.
  3. Take one reversible action first: remove unused seats, downgrade a tier, pause an add-on or consolidate a duplicate capability where safe.
  4. Record the baseline cost before the change.
  5. Re-measure after 30 days and only count savings that actually occurred.
