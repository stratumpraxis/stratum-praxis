The cost leaks we look for first

Unused or weakly used subscriptionsTools that remain paid for even when their current workflow value is unclear or has disappeared.
Duplicate capabilityMultiple products covering the same writing, meeting, automation, analytics, storage or workflow need.
AI add-on creepPremium AI features, usage charges or higher tiers added without a clear value baseline.
Over-sized plansSeats, tiers, credits or usage commitments that exceed what the team actually needs.
Renewal inertiaSubscriptions approaching renewal without a documented keep, reduce, replace or cancel decision.
Bad automation economicsAI workflows that cost more to run or maintain than the time, capacity or revenue value they create.
