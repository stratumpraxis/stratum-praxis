The decision

Do not ask only “Which tool is best?” Ask “Which spend still earns its place?”

The useful output is an explicit decision for each relevant subscription: KEEP when workflow value is clear, REDUCE when the plan is oversized, CONSOLIDATE when capability overlaps, REVIEW when evidence is weak, or CANCEL when cost no longer earns its place.

Renewal inertia

A subscription renews because nobody converted usage and workflow value into a keep/change decision.

Duplicate capability

Multiple products cover the same writing, meeting, automation, analytics or AI-assistant need.

Variable AI cost

Tokens, credits and usage-based charges make annual cost harder to predict than fixed seats.

Weak internal proof

The buyer likes the tool but cannot defend the number to finance before purchase or renewal.
