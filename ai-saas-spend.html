Where the friction is

The decision is not “which tool is best?” It is “which spend still earns its place?”

Renewal inertia

A subscription renews because nobody has converted usage and workflow value into a keep/change decision.

Duplicate capability

Multiple products perform overlapping writing, meeting, automation, analytics or AI-assistant functions.

Variable AI cost

Token, credit and usage-based charges make the real annual cost harder to predict than a flat software seat.

Weak internal proof

The buyer can find software quickly but still needs a credible ROI and risk case before approval or renewal.
