    Why 72 hours?

    Ready.gov uses at least three days of basic supplies as a baseline. The American Red Cross recommends a three-day go-kit and a longer stay-at-home supply. Treat 72 hours as a starting point, not a promise that help will arrive by then.

    Make it fit your household

    Adjust for local hazards, climate, pets, infants, disability and mobility needs, medical equipment, medications and any other dependency specific to your home.
