Why now

The European Commission states that Article 50 transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026. The rules cover direct AI interaction, marking and detection of AI-generated or manipulated content, deepfakes, certain public-interest text, and other transparency scenarios. The Commission also published implementation guidelines in July 2026 and an assessed Code of Practice for AI-generated-content transparency.
