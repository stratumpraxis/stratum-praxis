Safety & human approval boundaries

Never use infinite retries. Stop after repeated failures and diagnose. Require human review for account creation, irreversible publishing, legal or financial claims, sending messages to real people, high-frequency external actions, suspicious login challenges, CAPTCHA, or any action that could trigger platform enforcement.

7-day implementation

  1. Day 1: collect 10 market signals.
  2. Day 2: score and select one problem.
  3. Day 3: build the minimum paid outcome.
  4. Day 4: publish the offer and checkout.
  5. Day 5: connect one organic acquisition path.
  6. Day 6: inspect funnel data and buyer friction.
  7. Day 7: improve one bottleneck only.
