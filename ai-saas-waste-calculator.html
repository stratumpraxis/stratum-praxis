3. Before you ask finance to review this

Common questions before sharing a number upward.

What counts as "shadow AI" here?

Any AI tool or add-on paid for (personally expensed, on a team card, or bundled into another tool) that never went through the same review as your other software. Include it in the monthly figure above even if no one formally approved it — that is usually where the biggest surprise sits.

Is this just a subscription tracker?

No. It does not connect to your accounts, does not store what you enter, and does not claim to detect unused licenses automatically. It only turns a spend figure into a review-size decision, so you know whether the next step is worth taking.

Do I need procurement or IT before I use this?

No. Nothing here requires a login, an integration, or approval to try. It is meant to be the step you take before looping anyone else in, so you arrive at that conversation with a number instead of a guess.

What if the reducible amount comes out to $0?

That is a legitimate result, not a failure of the tool. The reducible share is your own scenario, not a promise — some stacks genuinely have little to trim once revenue-protecting or workflow-critical tools are set aside.
