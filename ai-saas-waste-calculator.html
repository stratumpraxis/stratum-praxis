What to inspect before cutting anything

  1. Unused or weakly used subscriptions.
  2. Duplicate capability across writing, meetings, automation, analytics, storage or AI assistants.
  3. Seats, tiers or credits larger than actual usage.
  4. AI add-ons that increased cost without a measurable value baseline.
  5. Renewals without a named owner or explicit decision date.
  6. Automations whose operating cost exceeds the time, capacity or revenue value they create.

Turn the number into a decision
