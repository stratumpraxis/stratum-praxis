PROCESS

OPERATIONS FIRST

Does the workflow still work after deployment?

A successful demo is not operating evidence. Examine one recurring process, including exceptions and recovery, before approving more automation.

01 · OWNERSHIP

Who owns each handoff?

Name the trigger, accountable owner, next recipient and the point where a human must approve consequential actions.

02 · DETECTION

How is failure noticed?

Identify missing events, stale queues, duplicate attempts, silent skips and mismatched payment or delivery states. Require an observable signal.

03 · TRIAGE

Who chooses the response?

Separate retryable errors from cases requiring human judgment. Define escalation owners, priority and safe-stop conditions.

04 · RECOVERY

Can the process recover safely?

Review event IDs, deduplication, bounded retries, rollback and manual fallback. Never replay irreversible actions blindly.

05 · EVIDENCE

What proves completion?

Record verified outcomes, delivery confirmation, unresolved exceptions and a concrete next review decision.

This checklist borrows the monitor → triage → respond → review logic common in managed operations. The Stratum $499 fixed-scope Workflow Audit is a one-workflow written decision service. It is not a 24/7 SOC, a managed security service, implementation or a guarantee of results.
