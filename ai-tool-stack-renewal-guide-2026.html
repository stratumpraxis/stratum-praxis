The decision is not “AI or no AI.”

For each paid tool or AI add-on, force one of five explicit decisions:

  • KEEP — clear owner, clear workflow, clear value.
  • REDUCE — useful, but seats, tier, credits or scope are oversized.
  • CONSOLIDATE — another paid tool already covers most of the capability.
  • REVIEW — value may exist, but the evidence is not good enough yet.
  • CANCEL — no owner, no meaningful usage, no critical dependency and no defensible business case.

What to review before spending more

  1. List paid AI tools, SaaS subscriptions and embedded AI add-ons.
  2. Attach one named owner and one real workflow to each item.
  3. Check actual usage, paid capacity, renewal timing and fallback.
  4. Group tools by capability, not vendor name, to expose overlap.
  5. Estimate rough break-even using annual cost and time/revenue value.
  6. Make the safest reversible decisions first: seat reduction, downgrade, consolidation test or a 30-day review window.
  7. Only then buy another tool or commission implementation.
